Word recording studio

Record each practice word in your own voice. For staff use only; this page is not linked from the student site.

Before you start
  • Use a quiet room. Hold the same distance from the microphone every time.
  • Press Record (or the space bar). Wait for “Say it now,” say the word once, then stay quiet. The page stops by itself, so there is no button click in the recording.
  • Press Play to listen. Press Keep & next (Enter) if it is good, or Redo.
  • Your takes are saved in this browser as you go. If the page closes, reopen it and continue.
